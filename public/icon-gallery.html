Chelo icon system — duotone with luminous accents

Every glyph is built from three layers: a translucent tint body, a crisp 1.8px line, and a single luminous accent (purple→cyan gradient with a soft glow). Accents brighten and scale on hover, halo behind the active sidebar item, and breathe while AI drafting, research or sync is in flight. Motion is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
